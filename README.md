# CDL-Training
Studying information 

## California CDL Class A Study App

**Open the app:** https://slimeshadie.github.io/CDL-Training/

Free study app for the California Class A CDL knowledge tests (General Knowledge, Air Brakes, Combination) and the HazMat, Tanker and Doubles/Triples endorsements, plus pre-trip and skills test prep.

- 2,958 practice questions (257 new in v22, 20 new in v19), each with a quick answer, a full explanation and an Evidence section quoting the California Commercial Driver Handbook (DL 650 / DL 650 X), 49 CFR or the California Vehicle Code
- Flashcards with spaced repetition (including HazMat placard and Do Not Load cards), timed practice tests, mixed practice, confidence ratings, progress stats
- Audio pre-trip walkthrough of every DL 650 inspection item with self-scoring, and a placard / hazard class / Do Not Load picture drill
- Readiness for each test (General Knowledge, Air Brakes, Combination, HazMat, Tanker, Doubles/Triples), showing "Ready to book" at 80+
- Study tools: a mistake journal (tag why you missed a question, then drill by reason), pretest mode with pretest vs. post-test scores, two-step hints, an optional "explain it in your own words" box, and practice test history charts with the 80% pass line
- Real test prep: California CDL test formats with official sources, DMV-format practice exams (question count, misses allowed, 3 answer choices, handbook topic weights), skills test guide (modernized vehicle inspection, basic control exercises, road test rules), CLP rules and restriction codes, California size and weight limits, and the CDL test sites
- Worked examples with calculators (following and stopping distance, axle weights and the bridge table, kingpin-to-rear-axle, air brake numbers), ordering, matching, fill-in and scenario exercises, an audio playlist for hands-free review, and read-aloud study guides
- English language proficiency questions and a Reference section (49 CFR 391.11(b)(2), FMCSA roadside policy, California DMV 2026 rules)
- **Similar questions & study material** (🔎 button, or press L) on every question before and after you answer, every flashcard, guide section, diagram and test review. Related items come from a topic map of 81 concepts that was checked by hand, so the same rule or idea comes first
- **Related topics** at the end of every explanation: topic page links and 5 questions on the same topic to try right away
- **Topic explorer** tab (and a card on the Start page): 81 topics grouped by test; each topic page has a concept summary, key numbers, common mistakes, all questions, flashcards, guide facts and tables, diagrams, worked examples, exercises, glossary terms and handbook subsections, with Practice all, Flashcards and Mini test buttons
- **Rule breakdown** and **Step-by-step breakdown** in explanations, plus a **Rules & numbers** reference: 43 rule cards and 15 procedure cards with verbatim DL 650 / 49 CFR / CA DMV wording and citations, key numbers, who it applies to, exceptions, penalties, California vs federal differences, recent changes, “similar rules people confuse” tables, official steps with why each matters, common mistakes and automatic fails. Items where sources disagree are marked “check before test day”
- **Same fact and Same topic lists (v22):** after you answer, and in the Similar questions dialog, related questions are split into **Same fact** (only questions that test exactly the same fact, rule or procedure, plus hand-linked neighbouring facts marked *Linked fact*; the list can be short or empty, with a button to practice the wider subtopic instead) and a separately labelled **Same topic — broader practice** list. 257 new original questions (CV 68, AB 59, HM 58, TV 40, DT 32), written from DL 650 / DL 650 X wording and marked “new in v22”, fill in facts that had fewer than 5 questions (404 such facts in v21, 301 now; none left in Tanker or Doubles/Triples). Each new question has the full rule, why each wrong choice is wrong, and verbatim Evidence quotes.
- **Same fact (v21):** 2,465 of the 2,701 questions are also grouped by hand into 587 fact groups — questions that test exactly the same fact, rule or procedure, even across different tests (for example the spring-brake “fail-safe” idea in Air Brakes and General Knowledge). Similar questions and the Try-these list put those first and mark them **Same fact**, then linked facts, near-duplicates, the same subtopic and the same topic (ordered by shared words plus meaning similarity). Explanations add a “Practice all on this fact” button. Flashcards, study-guide facts, diagrams, worked examples, exercises and glossary entries list the questions on the fact they teach first, and each question’s related flashcards and guide facts drop weak matches.
- **Subtopics (v20):** every question belongs to one of 230 hand-checked subtopics (one fact, rule or procedure each, such as “Low air pressure warning (55–75 psi, wig wag)” or “Tractor protection valve”). Similar questions and the Try-these list in each explanation come from the same subtopic first, explanations show the subtopic with a Practice button, and each topic page lists its subtopics with their questions. Rule and procedure cards are attached only to questions whose subtopic the card covers
- Updates show a clear banner and load by themselves when you are not in the middle of a practice or test
- v18 features: More like this on every question, flashcard, guide section, diagram and test review: similar questions, related flashcards, guide facts, diagrams, worked examples, exercises and glossary terms, with one-tap practice, flashcards or a mini test on that topic
- Today’s review on the Start page (spaced repetition for questions and flashcards, daily goal, streak), a study session builder, a topic mastery map, notes on any question or guide with search, plain-language summaries and key takeaways on each guide, printable one-page summaries, and end-of-session recaps
- Keyboard shortcuts you can change or turn off (press ? for the list): answer with 1–4 or A–D, N/P or arrows for next and previous, X skip, H hint, E explanation, V evidence, F flag, R read aloud, L more like this, M then 1–5 mistake reason, G then a letter to jump to any tab, Ctrl/Cmd+K command palette, auto-advance after a correct answer; flashcard swipe and optional vibration
- A "Report a problem" link on every question that opens a prefilled GitHub issue
- Works offline after the first visit and can be installed to your home screen
- Accessibility settings (button in the header or Alt+Shift+A): text size, built-in Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic fonts (SIL Open Font License, see [fonts/](fonts/)), line height, letter and word spacing, line width, color-blind-safe mode, system/light/dark theme, high contrast, reduced motion, 1.5×/2×/untimed tests, larger tap targets, strong focus outline, focus mode, reading ruler, read-aloud voice, speed and pitch with captions and word highlighting, and screen-reader announcements

**Android app (test build):** [downloads/ca-cdl-study.apk](downloads/ca-cdl-study.apk) – version 21, debug-signed. It installs over versions 12 to 20 and keeps your progress. If you have an older test build that will not update, export your progress (My stats → export), uninstall it, then install this one.
